In an effort to move towards the trajectory generation on the biped, try to complete (or at least look through) the [Resolved Rate](https://pvela.gatech.edu/classes/doku.php?id=ece4560:biped:04rrinvkin) and [Optimal Control](https://pvela.gatech.edu/classes/doku.php?id=ece4560:biped:05optcntrl) modules with the main goal of generating a joint-configuration trajectory (set of joint angles over time) for the biped.
